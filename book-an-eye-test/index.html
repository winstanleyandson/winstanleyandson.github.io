What happens at your appointment

  1. Book a time that suits you

Common questions

Booking an eye test, answered

Do I need to book an eye test?

Yes, please book so we can give you proper, unhurried time. Repairs, adjustments and glazing do not need an appointment; just call in.

How much does an eye test cost?

A private eye examination is £45. NHS sight tests are free if you qualify, and we check your entitlement for you when you arrive. If you are buying varifocals this month, the examination is free with the varifocal offer whether or not you qualify for the NHS. Glasses Club members get their annual eye test discounted or free, depending on their plan.

How long does an eye test take?

Allow around 30 minutes. We do not rush it, and you leave understanding what was found.

Will I see the same optician each time?

Yes. It is the same two of us every visit, so we know your eyes and your history. You are never passed to a different person each time you come in.

Can I book an eye test for my child?

Yes. Under-16s get a free NHS sight test. Pick any time in the calendar and add your child’s name in the notes. If your child is short-sighted, ask about Stellest myopia control lenses.

Can I book an eye test for today or tomorrow?

Often, yes. The calendar shows live availability, so if a slot is there, it is yours. For anything urgent, call us on 0151 726 0622 and we will fit you in where we can.

Do you do eye tests for driving?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we allow the time. If you need to show a driving standard of vision, we can confirm it after the examination.

What should I bring?

Your current glasses, any recent prescription, and a note of any medication you take. If you think you qualify for an NHS sight test, bring proof of the benefit or exemption if you have it.

Where do I park?

Free on Park Road right outside the shop, and free at Tesco Extra opposite with no time limit. The entrance is step-free.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Text or WhatsApp us, or call 0151 726 0622, and we will move it. As much notice as you can manage helps us offer the slot to someone else.
