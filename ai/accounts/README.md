# Accounts

Twelve AI accounts designed on 17 October 2026 for Winstanley & Son, a frame maker in Dingle, Liverpool: four leadership roles, an advisory board and seven operational accounts. The full design is in [ROSTER.md](ROSTER.md). Every account inherits the [shared operating rules](_shared/operating-rules.md).

None of them replaces a live system. They collect, check, flag and draft. The owner is the only person who decides and presses.

## Reporting lines

The owner sits at the top. The AI CEO, AI CMO, AI CIO, AI CFO and the advisory board report to the owner. The close-out chaser reports to the AI CEO and the content drafter to the AI CMO. The rules librarian, data hygiene auditor, build and site verifier and instruction auditor report to the AI CIO. The model and cost ledger reports to the AI CFO and writes a line into the AI CIO's agents register.

## Handoff map

- Close-out chaser: tally to the AI CEO; sale-value and attendance counts to the AI CMO.
- Data hygiene auditor: register lines to the AI CIO; cohort counts to the AI CMO; a failed postcode check blocks a mail send.
- Rules librarian: approved wording to the content drafter; compliance dates to the AI CIO.
- Content drafter: public drafts to the owner; edit rates to the AI CMO.
- Build and site verifier: pass or block to the build agent; passed packages to Codex.
- Instruction auditor and model and cost ledger: one page to the AI CIO and a cost line to the AI CFO, with flags to the owner.
- AI CFO, AI CMO and AI CIO send figures to the AI CEO with source, date and a proven-or-reported flag. The AI CEO feeds the advisory board and sends the weekly pack to the owner.

## Three waves

1. **Wave 1, now to end of November 2026:** AI CFO, AI CIO, build and site verifier, model and cost ledger. All read-only. They give every later account its kill switch, run gate and cost line, so a mistake costs a wrong report, not a wrong press.
2. **Wave 2, December 2026 to February 2027:** AI CMO, close-out chaser, data hygiene auditor, content drafter, rules librarian. They fill the named measurement gaps (sale values, attendance, recall response) and give the first measurable drafting work.
3. **Wave 3, March 2027 onwards:** AI CEO, advisory board, instruction auditor. They argue from numbers, so they start once the ledgers hold measured figures. The facts-pack script is built in wave 1.

## Open owner decisions

- When does the AI CEO start: when measured lines outnumber unmeasured ones, or on a fixed date?
- Where is the rules librarian's wiki held, given it stays outside this public repository?
- Should a "how did you hear about us" field be added, and who enters it?
- Who approves any staff or customer likeness before the content drafter's work is used?
- Does the owner want any model switch to need a written approval, as the ledger proposes?
