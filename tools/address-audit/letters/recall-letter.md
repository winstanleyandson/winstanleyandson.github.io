# Recall letter (bands: due30, 0-6m)

Use when a patient's eye examination is due or up to six months overdue and the phone
has failed, or as the standard postal recall. Merge fields use GHL syntax.
Sender block, date and address block are supplied by the Stannp / Scribeless template.

---

Dear {{contact.first_name}},

Your eye examination with us is now due.

Our records show your last examination was on {{contact.last_exam_date}}, and we
recommended you come back around now. We tried to reach you by phone on
{{contact.phone}} but couldn't get through, so we're writing instead.

A regular eye examination does more than check whether your prescription has changed.
It lets us look at the health of your eyes and pick up early signs of conditions such as
glaucoma, cataracts and macular degeneration, most of which are far easier to manage when
they're found early. Even if your glasses feel fine, it's worth keeping to the interval.

Booking is simple. You can:

- Call us on 0151 726 0622 (Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm)
- Book online at winstanleyandson.co.uk/book
- Reply to this letter, or call in at 252 Park Road any weekday

There's free parking outside the practice and at Tesco Extra opposite.

If you've had your eyes tested elsewhere since we last saw you, or your phone number has
changed, please let us know so we can keep your record up to date and stop chasing you
unnecessarily.

We look forward to seeing you soon.
