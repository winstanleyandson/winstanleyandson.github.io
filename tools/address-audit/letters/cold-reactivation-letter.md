# Cold reactivation letter (bands: 12-24m, 24-36m)

Use when a patient is one to three years overdue. Assume they may have moved practice,
lost touch, or simply forgotten. Tone is warm and unhurried, no pressure, with a clear
reason to come back and an easy way to opt out. Run deceased screening and the PAF
cleanse before any send to this band.
