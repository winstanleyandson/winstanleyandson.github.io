Common questions

The Glasses Club, answered

What does the Glasses Club cover?

Repair or replacement of your glasses when they are scratched, damaged, lost or stolen, depending on your plan. The original prescription, frame model and colour are replaced, or the closest alternative if that frame is no longer made.

Is there an excess?

Yes. 25% of the original price for repairs and replacements, and 50% when the item is lost or stolen and cannot be returned to us. If both frame and lenses are replaced at once, the excess applies to each.

How do the savings plans work?

Bronze, Silver and Gold add £10, £20 or £30 a month on top of your plan. After 12 months we add a bonus, 10% on Basic, 12.5% on Pro and 17.5% on VIP, and the whole amount is yours to spend on your next glasses or sunglasses. Credits must be used within 30 days of your 12th month.

Do I need to be an existing patient?

No. Anyone can join. Bring your current glasses in so we can record the prescription, frame and lenses being covered.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no contract. Cancel at any time with 30 days' notice and your benefits run to the end of that billing period. Membership fees are not refunded.

Who is covered?

The named person on the membership only. Benefits and credits cannot be transferred, and we may ask for ID when you claim or redeem credit.

The small print

Glasses Club terms

Read the full terms and conditions

1. Membership benefits

The Glasses Club provides eligible members with access to exclusive benefits, including discounted replacement of glasses, priority access to new products, and other perks as detailed at the time of sign-up.

2. Excess fees for replacements

A 25% excess fee, based on the original retail price, applies to all replacement frames or lenses. A 50% excess fee applies to any item being replaced that is not returned to the store, including those that are lost or stolen. Replacement of both frame and lenses at the same time will incur the appropriate excess fee for each component individually.

3. Replacement criteria

Only the original prescription provided at the time of the original order will be covered for replacement. Only the original frame model and colour will be replaced, subject to availability. If the original model or colour is no longer available, we will offer the closest available alternative at our discretion.

4. Credit usage

Annual membership credits must be used within 30 days following the 12th month of the membership start date. Unused credits will expire and cannot be carried over into the following year.

5. Named person only

Glasses Club benefits and credits are strictly non-transferable and apply only to the named person on the membership. ID may be required to redeem credits or request replacements.

6. Right to withdraw membership

Winstanley & Son reserves the right to refuse or cancel membership to any individual at our sole discretion and without obligation to provide justification, and to withdraw access to the Glasses Club or change the terms at any time, with reasonable notice where appropriate.

7. General

Membership fees are non-refundable unless otherwise stated in writing. These terms may be updated from time to time; the latest version will always be available on request or via our website. By continuing membership, you agree to any revised terms. Cancellation requires 30 days' notice; benefits cease at the end of the billing period.
