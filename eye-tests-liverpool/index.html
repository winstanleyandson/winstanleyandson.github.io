Common questions

Eye tests, answered

How much is an eye test?

Private eye examinations are charged; NHS sight tests are free if you qualify. Ask when you book and we will tell you which applies to you.

Am I entitled to a free NHS eye test?

You are if you are over 60, under 16 (or under 19 in full-time education), diabetic, have glaucoma or a close relative with it, are registered blind or partially sighted, or receive certain benefits. We will check for you.

How long does the examination take?

Allow around 30 minutes. We do not rush it.

What does the eye test check?

Your vision and prescription, the health of the front and back of your eyes, eye pressure where appropriate, and how your eyes work together. You get a clear explanation, not a sales pitch.

Do I have to buy glasses from you?

No. You are given your prescription and are free to take it anywhere. Most people stay because of the fitting and the frames.
